================ Part 1a: single-threaded ================

--- Missing arguments ---
$ ./bin/cflask

--- Terminal 1 (server) ---
$ ./bin/cflask s 8080
cflask [single-threaded] on http://0.0.0.0:8080

--- Terminal 2 (client) ---
$ curl -i http://127.0.0.1:8080/
HTTP/1.1 200 OK
Content-Type: text/plain
Content-Length: 11
Connection: close

Hello World
$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/square

$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/cube

$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/helloworld

$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/pingpong

$ ps -o nlwp= -p $(pgrep -x cflask)

================ Part 1b: thread per request ================

--- Terminal 1 (server) ---
$ ./bin/cflask m 8080 64
cflask [thread per request, max 64 threads] on http://0.0.0.0:8080

--- Terminal 2 (client) ---
$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/square
1
$ curl http://127.0.0.1:8080/helloworld

--- Threads while idle ---
$ ps -o nlwp= -p $(pgrep -x cflask)

$ for i in 1 2 3 4 5; do ps -o nlwp= -p $(pgrep -x cflask); sleep 0.5; done

1

--- Threads after the load ends ---
$ ps -o nlwp= -p $(pgrep -x cflask)
